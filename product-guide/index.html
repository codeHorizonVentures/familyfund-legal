Monthly category budgets

1. Choose a category limit

Set an amount for a spending category such as groceries. Limits express your plan for the month.

2. Save expenses

Record spending against its category. A saved transaction updates the financial records used by your budget views.

3. Review the balance

Compare recorded spending with the category limit. Remaining budget is based on the records you enter; it is not a bank balance or a guarantee that you can afford a purchase.

For example, a fictional €500 grocery limit with €320 of recorded spending leaves €180 of that category budget. Missing or incorrect transactions affect the result.

Sharing is an explicit CloudKit workflow

FamilyFund implements household invitations and participant access through Apple CloudKit sharing. Private and shared records use separate CloudKit database scopes. Personal iCloud sync alone does not invite another person or grant access to their records.

Participants can access the shared records permitted by the share. Treat invitations as access to household information. Revoking access cannot recall exports or copies someone already made. Availability also depends on Apple account and CloudKit configuration.

Receipt recognition creates a draft for review

Capture or select

Use the camera with permission, or select a receipt image. Camera permission is separate from analytics consent.

Recognise and check

Apple Vision and the bundled receipt-recognition library run on device. They can suggest the amount, currency, date and merchant. Check and correct the suggestions.

Confirm and save

Confirmed fields become a financial record when you save. The current flow does not attach the original receipt image to the saved transaction. Recognition success does not certify accurate fields.

Reporting a recognition issue by email is a separate, voluntary action. Review the draft and any receipt attachment before sending it to support.

Privacy has separate controls

Financial records stay on your device and, when enabled, in Apple iCloud. Optional product analytics excludes financial and receipt contents.

Analytics requires affirmative consent to the current notice. You can decline without losing budgeting or receipt recognition, and change your choice in Profile > Privacy controls. Withdrawing consent stops future capture and cancels active uploads where possible; it does not recall requests already received.

Apple handles subscription billing. Exchange-rate requests and voluntary support email involve their respective providers. Read the data-handling overview and full privacy policy for details.
