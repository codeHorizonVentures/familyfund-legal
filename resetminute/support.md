  <h1>ResetMinute support</h1>
  <p class="lede">ResetMinute helps desk workers take short breaks for neck, shoulder, and back discomfort during long workdays. The main flow is quick guided desk resets, with optional reminders and optional posture check-ins.</p>

  <h2>Contact</h2>

  <p>Support email: <a href="mailto:support@familyfund.app">support@familyfund.app</a></p>
  <p>This inbox also handles ResetMinute support during the MVP release. ResetMinute support pages are hosted on <code>familyfund.app</code> for the MVP, but the product and support content on this path applies to ResetMinute only.</p>

  <h2>How reminders work</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>ResetMinute uses local notifications on your iPhone.</li>
    <li>You can choose weekday reminders, start time, end time, and interval.</li>
    <li>If notification access is not granted, reminders will not be scheduled until you enable them in iOS Settings.</li>
    <li>If you previously denied notifications, re-enable them in <strong>Settings &gt; Notifications &gt; ResetMinute</strong>. Reminders stay off until access is restored.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>How optional check-ins work</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>The camera is used only for optional check-ins.</li>
    <li>Check-ins are secondary. You can use ResetMinute without making the camera your main workflow.</li>
    <li>Images and pose-related artifacts stay on your device and are only saved locally when you choose to save a journal entry.</li>
    <li>If camera access was denied, you can re-enable it in <strong>Settings &gt; Privacy &amp; Security &gt; Camera</strong> or in the ResetMinute app settings on your iPhone.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>How to remove saved journal entries</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>You can delete saved entries from the journal inside the app.</li>
    <li>Deleting an entry removes its local journal record and any saved check-in photos or pose-related data stored with that entry.</li>
    <li>If an entry includes saved photos, you can also clear those photos from the entry detail view without deleting the whole entry.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Known MVP limits</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>ResetMinute is iPhone-first only.</li>
    <li>The app is a general-wellness desk reset tool, not a diagnostic or treatment product.</li>
    <li>Optional check-ins are intended for posture awareness only and should not be treated as clinical assessment.</li>
    <li>The current MVP keeps reminder settings and saved journal data on device and does not use a ResetMinute-operated backend.</li>
    <li>Deleting the app removes locally stored journal data from that device.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
