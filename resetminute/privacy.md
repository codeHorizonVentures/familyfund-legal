<section class="content-panel">
  <p>ResetMinute is a general-wellness app for desk workers. For privacy and data protection purposes, ResetMinute is operated by <strong>Petro Kulakov</strong>.</p>

  <p>This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ResetMinute or contact support.</p>

  <h2>1. Data processed in the app</h2>

  <p>ResetMinute may process the following categories of information on your device:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>reminder settings such as weekday schedule, workday window, and reminder interval</li>
    <li>journal entries you choose to save</li>
    <li>optional check-in images saved as part of a journal entry</li>
    <li>optional pose-related data saved as part of a journal entry</li>
    <li>basic app preferences such as onboarding state</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>2. Camera use</h2>

  <p>ResetMinute uses the camera only for optional check-ins. The app’s main daily value is short desk reset routines, not camera scanning.</p>

  <p>Check-in images and pose-related artifacts stay on your device and are only saved locally if you choose to save a journal entry.</p>

  <h2>3. Reminder notifications</h2>

  <p>ResetMinute uses Apple local notification APIs to schedule reminder notifications on your device. Reminder schedules are managed locally on your iPhone.</p>

  <h2>4. Storage and transfers</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>ResetMinute does not operate its own backend for storing your journal entries, images, or reminder configuration.</li>
    <li>The current MVP is designed around local, on-device storage.</li>
    <li>We do not intentionally upload your journal entries, check-in images, or pose-related artifacts to our own servers.</li>
    <li>The current MVP does not require account creation or cloud sync.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>5. Support communications</h2>

  <p>If you contact support, we may process the information you choose to send, such as your email address, screenshots, attachments, and app-version details needed to respond.</p>

  <h2>6. Data retention</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>saved journal entries remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app</li>
    <li>deleting a journal entry removes that local journal record and any saved check-in photos or pose-related data stored with that entry</li>
    <li>if an entry includes saved photos, those photos can also be cleared separately from the entry detail view</li>
    <li>deleting the app removes locally stored ResetMinute data from that device</li>
    <li>support emails and attachments are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond and handle the support case</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>7. Your controls</h2>

  <p>You can control your information directly by deleting saved journal entries and by changing notification permissions in iOS settings.</p>

  <h2>8. Contact</h2>

  <p>For privacy questions or support requests, contact <a href="mailto:support@familyfund.app">support@familyfund.app</a>.</p>
</section>
